1. Introduction & Spiritual Reverence

Creative Bliss Labs is dedicated to serving the global Sikh Sangat with an authentic, respectful, and distraction-free digital Gurbani companion. We firmly uphold user privacy, data security, and transparency as core spiritual and operational values.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application accesses, collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use Bani: Nitnem & Steek Companion across supported devices.

2. Core Philosophy: Offline-First & Privacy by Design

  • 100% Offline-First Gurbani Core: All Gurmukhi Gurbani texts, Steek translations (English, Punjabi, and Spanish), transliterations, and essential audio files are bundled locally on your device. Your sacred reading and listening experience never requires an active internet connection.
  • No Tracking of Scripture Reading: Your personal recitation habits, listening history, bookmarks, and Nitnem habits remain strictly stored on your local device. We do not monitor, profile, log, or sell what Banis or Shabads you read or listen to.

3. Information We Collect and Purpose of Processing

We collect only the minimum data necessary to deliver the features you choose to use:

1. Spiritual Milestones & Backup (Optional)

If you optionally choose to sign in (via Google Firebase Authentication), we store non-sensitive spiritual milestones—such as your total XP, active streak count, completed Banis for the day, and Sangat group leaderboard profile—in Google Cloud Firestore solely to enable cloud synchronization and device recovery. If you choose not to sign in, all progress data remains strictly on your local device.

2. In-App Purchases & Voluntary Seva Support

In-app purchases, subscriptions, and voluntary Seva patron memberships are securely handled through the Google Play Store and processed using RevenueCat billing infrastructure. Creative Bliss Labs never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card, debit card, or banking details. RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase receipts and provide us solely with anonymous entitlement tokens to grant your tier status.

3. Diagnostics, Performance & Analytics

Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics, crash reports, and performance logs are collected through Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play Vitals to identify application faults and ensure uninterrupted media playback across various device models and Android versions.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers to deliver cloud features, diagnostics, and subscription handling. These providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (Purchase validation and entitlement status): https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Data Selling

  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: Bani: Nitnem & Steek Companion contains no third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network). The app is 100% ad-free and distraction-free.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, spiritual journey, or account credentials to any data brokers, ad networks, or commercial entities.

6. Device Permissions & Purpose

Bani: Nitnem & Steek Companion requests only essential permissions strictly aligned with app functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Solely utilized to deliver scheduled Amritvela, Rehras Sahib, and Kirtan Sohila Nitnem reminders configured by you.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android system architecture to maintain uninterrupted audio playback of Nitnem Banis while your screen is turned off or while using other applications.

7. Data Security & Encryption

We take the protection of your data seriously. All communications between the app and external cloud servers (such as Firebase and RevenueCat) are securely encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols, including HTTPS / TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • Local Data: You can reset or wipe all local app data directly from Settings > Privacy & Data Management > Clear Local Data, or by clearing app storage in Android device settings.
  • In-App Cloud Account Deletion: If you created an account, you can request complete account and data deletion directly within the app settings.
  • Web Deletion Request: In accordance with Google Play requirements, you may also request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data without reinstalling the app by by emailing creativeblisslabs@gmail.com. All associated records will be permanently erased within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Bani: Nitnem & Steek Companion contains sacred spiritual content suitable for all ages and families. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.
