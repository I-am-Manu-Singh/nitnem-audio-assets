1. Introduction & Spiritual Reverence

Nitnem Steek is dedicated to serving the global Sikh Sangat with an authentic, respectful, and distraction-free digital Gurbani companion. We firmly uphold user privacy and transparency as core spiritual values.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you interact with Nitnem Gutka Steek.

2. Core Philosophy: Offline-First & Privacy by Design

  • 100% Offline-First Gurbani Core: All Gurmukhi Gurbani texts, Steek translations (English, Punjabi, and Spanish), transliterations, and essential audio files are bundled locally on your device. Your sacred reading and listening experience never requires internet connectivity.
  • No Tracking of Scripture Reading: Your personal reading, listening, recitation history, bookmarks, and Nitnem habits remain strictly stored on your local device. We do not collect, track, or upload your spiritual journey data.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the absolute minimum data necessary to provide essential cloud features:

1. Spiritual Milestones & Backup (Optional)

If you optionally choose to sign in (via Google Firebase Authentication), we store non-sensitive spiritual milestones—such as your total XP, active streak count, completed Banis for the day—solely to enable optional cloud synchronization across devices. Your email address is used only for account recovery and is never shared with third parties.

2. In-App Purchases & Voluntary Seva Support

In-app purchases, subscriptions, and voluntary Seva patron memberships are securely processed directly by the Google Play Store (via RevenueCat billing infrastructure). Creative Bliss Labs never directly handles or stores your payment card information.

3. Crash Diagnostics & Performance (Anonymous)

Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics and crash logs may be collected through standard Google Play Vitals to identify bugs and ensure uninterrupted media playback across varying device hardware and Android versions. No personally identifiable information is included in diagnostic reports.

4. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Data Selling

  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: Nitnem Gutka Steek has zero third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network). The app is 100% ad-free and distraction-free.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, spiritual journey, or account credentials to any data brokers or advertisers.

5. Device Permissions & Purpose

Nitnem Gutka Steek requests only essential permissions strictly aligned with app functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Solely utilized to deliver scheduled Amritvela, Rehras Sahib, and Kirtan Sohila Nitnem reminders configured by you.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android system architecture to maintain uninterrupted audio playback of Nitnem Banis while your screen is turned off or while using other applications.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data:

  • You can reset or wipe all local app data directly from Settings > Privacy & Data Management > Clear Local Data.
  • If you have an active cloud account and wish to permanently erase all associated cloud records (email, streak history, leaderboard profile), you can request immediate deletion directly via email to creativeblisslabs@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Nitnem Gutka Steek contains sacred spiritual content suitable for all ages and families. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
